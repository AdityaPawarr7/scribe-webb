## Observations by section

### Context
Studio-dominant week. Visual, hands-on work; little screen-based research. Cares about every class except theoretical ones.

### During a crit
Does **not** write during the crit — chooses to listen fully instead. Writes afterwards from memory. Trades off capture completeness for presence in the moment. Memory "sometimes" fails.

### Revisions tracking
Uses a **physical dated diary**. Finds entries by flipping. Not frustrated by this. Paper works for her for the capture step; the pain is elsewhere.

### Going back to older notes
Not probed as deeply (she steered the conversation toward reminders instead). Implicit: paper diary → fine for recent, no mention of long-term retrieval.

### AI tools
Uses LLMs **as a tutor**, not a scribe: pastes material she doesn't understand and converses with the model to build understanding. Clear mental separation between "AI to think with" (yes) and "AI to record for me" (hasn't needed).

### Recording / privacy
Comfortable **if** the output is written notes and data stays local. Strong validation of the local-first stance.

### Closing — the real need
"Paperwork gets messy." She wants a **clean dashboard with reminders** — the organizational layer on top of the raw notes.

## Pain points (raw)
- Memory sometimes drops important points from crits.
- Paperwork (across projects, classes, deadlines) gets messy — no single organized view.
- No system that nudges her about things she wrote down.

## Signals for later steps
- **New requirement candidate:** reminders / deadlines surfaced from meeting notes (P1 unique).

- **Reinforced:** local-first privacy stance is a feature, not a trade.
- **Design implication:** a dashboard view (not just per-meeting view) belongs on the roadmap.
