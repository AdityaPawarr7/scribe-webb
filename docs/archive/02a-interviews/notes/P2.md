## Observations by section
### Context
Carries 5 classes; cares most about **automata** (whiteboard-heavy, concept-dense). Senior design is a "get through."

### Last important lecture
Whiteboard-driven. Prof draws machine diagrams and talks over them. P2 copies the diagram, which costs attention: he loses the verbal thread for a few seconds at a time.

### Catch-up loop
When he loses the thread, he doesn't ask the prof — he turns to the person next to him. Human cross-check, not a tool.

### After lecture
Notes are not touched between lectures. Reviewed only before exams, and often don't make sense when he gets there.

### Study fallback
Goes to YouTube to re-learn the concept generally. Explicit complaint: **"it is not personalized."** The content gap is between what his prof said and what a stranger on YouTube explains.

### AI tools
Has not used ChatGPT on class material and doesn't want to.

### Recording / privacy
Indifferent to being recorded in any class context.

### Closing surprise
Unprompted frustration: notes are **scattered across multiple notebooks** (physical, apps, screenshots?). He wants a **single fast reference**.

## Pain points (raw)
- Can't copy whiteboard diagrams AND track the explanation at the same time
- Notes don't make sense weeks later
- YouTube is generic — doesn't match the prof's framing
- Material lives in several places; no fast way to pull it up
- Low trust / low interest in AI chat tools out of the box
